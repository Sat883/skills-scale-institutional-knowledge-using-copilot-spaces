## Project Management Processes

OctoAcme's project management is anchored in customer-first, iterative delivery, clear role ownership, data-informed decision-making, and psychological safety, ensuring projects produce valuable, usable outcomes with adaptive learning at each stage.

### Our Roles
The team includes Project Manager (coordination, schedules, risk), Product Manager (outcomes, backlog prioritization), Scrum Master (agile facilitation, impediment removal), Business Analyst (requirements, stakeholder bridge), Developers (implementation, testability), QA/Testing (quality validation), UX/UI Designer (user-centric design), DevOps Engineer (deployment, infrastructure), and Stakeholders (inputs, approvals).

For detailed role descriptions and interaction patterns, see [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) and [Role-Responsibility Matrix](role-responsibility-matrix.md).

### Our Workflow
The core workflow follows a well-defined project lifecycle: **Initiation** (problem and stakeholder identification), **Planning** (scope, resourcing, milestones, dependencies), **Execution** (build, test, review, iterate), **Release** (deployment, verification, announcement), and **Close & Retrospective** (capture learnings for continuous improvement).

Key artifacts such as a project charter, roadmap, sprint backlog, acceptance criteria, and a risk register help maintain transparency and alignment across teams and stages.

### Communication & Quality
Communication at OctoAcme is proactive and regular, including weekly syncs between PM and PdM, daily or twice-weekly delivery team standups, sprint ceremonies, and monthly stakeholder updates, alongside ad-hoc check-ins for escalations. Quality assurance is embedded across the lifecycle, with QA/testing roles confirming acceptance criteria at every stage and retrospectives addressing gaps and driving process improvements.

For detailed communication guidelines, see [Risks and Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md).

## How to Use These Resources

### For Project Managers
- Use the **Project Kickoff Checklist** to ensure no step is missed when starting new projects
- Reference the **Role-Responsibility Matrix** to clarify ownership and delegate effectively
- Apply the **Handoff Checklist** when transitioning between project phases
- Maintain project artifacts (Charter, risk register, retros) per guidelines in process docs

### For Scrum Masters
- Use the **Retrospective Template** to facilitate effective sprint retrospectives
- Reference **Roles and Personas** to coach team members on collaboration patterns
- Drive continuous improvement using retrospective action items

### For Team Members (Developers, QA, UX/UI, DevOps, BA)
- Understand your role and responsibilities in **Roles and Personas**
- See your interaction points with other roles in the **Role-Responsibility Matrix**
- Use the **Handoff Checklist** when transitioning work to other team members
- Participate actively in retrospectives using the **Retrospective Template** structure

### For Product Managers & Business Analysts
- Reference **Project Initiation** for defining problem statements and success metrics
- Use **Roles and Personas** to understand stakeholder collaboration patterns
- Apply the **Role-Responsibility Matrix** to clarify backlog and requirements ownership

### For Stakeholders
- Review the **Project Management Overview** for a high-level understanding of our processes
- Understand your role and touchpoints in **Roles and Personas** and **Role-Responsibility Matrix**
- Expect regular communication per the cadence outlined in process docs

## Artifact Maintenance

To keep documentation valuable and current:

- **Project Charter**: Update when scope, timeline, or team changes; review monthly
- **Risk Register**: Review weekly; update as new risks emerge
- **Retrospective Notes**: Document after every retro; track action item completion
- **Process Documentation**: Review quarterly or when process gaps are identified
- **Templates**: Update based on retrospective feedback and team needs

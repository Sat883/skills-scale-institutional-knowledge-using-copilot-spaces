# OctoAcme — Retrospective & Continuous Improvement

## Purpose
Capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements. Retrospectives are essential quality assurance checkpoints that help us continuously improve our processes, communication, and deliverables.

## When
After each sprint, release, or important milestone. Also after incidents.

**Cadence Recommendations**:
- **Sprint retrospectives**: After every sprint (typically every 2 weeks)
- **Release retrospectives**: After major releases or deployments
- **Milestone retrospectives**: At the end of project phases (initiation, planning, execution)
- **Incident retrospectives**: Within 1-3 days after significant incidents or outages
- **Project closure retrospectives**: At project completion before team disbands

## Structure
- What went well
- What could be improved
- Action items (owner, due date)
- Follow-up on previous action items

## Running a Retrospective
- Timebox: 45–75 minutes depending on team size
- Use an anonymous idea board if needed to encourage candor
- Prioritize 2–3 top action items to avoid overload
- Create psychological safety by focusing on systems, not individuals
- Review progress on previous action items to ensure accountability

**Using the Retrospective Template**:
For a comprehensive, reusable retrospective structure, use the [Retrospective Template](retrospective-template.md). This template includes:
- Pre-retrospective preparation checklist
- Multiple activity format options (Timeline, Start/Stop/Continue, Glad/Sad/Mad)
- Action item tracking with success criteria
- Team health indicators and metrics
- Facilitator tips and best practices

## Tracking Improvements
- Add action items to the project backlog or issues with clear owners and timelines
- Review outstanding actions in the weekly PM sync
- Track completion rate of retrospective action items as a team health metric
- Celebrate completed improvements in the next retrospective

**Quality Assurance Integration**:
- Include QA/Testing representatives in retrospectives to surface quality-related issues
- Review quality metrics (bug escape rate, test coverage, deployment success rate)
- Identify quality process gaps and create action items to address them
- Track improvements in quality metrics sprint-over-sprint

**Artifact Maintenance**:
- Keep retrospective notes in a central location (docs/ folder or wiki)
- Update the Project Charter or process docs based on retrospective learnings
- Maintain a rolling log of action items and their outcomes
- Archive completed retrospectives for future reference and onboarding

## Continuous Improvement Culture
- Measure impact of action items
- Celebrate improvements and make small, iterative changes
- Encourage all team members to propose process improvements
- Maintain a backlog of improvement ideas for future retrospectives
- Review and update project artifacts (Charter, risk register, communication plan) based on learnings

## Communication and Follow-Through
**Retrospective Communication Plan**:
- Distribute retrospective notes within 24 hours to all participants
- Add action items to project board with "retrospective" or "process improvement" label
- Review action item progress in daily standups or weekly syncs
- Report on completed improvements to stakeholders in monthly updates

**Action Item Accountability**:
- Each action item must have a single owner
- Due dates should be within the next 1-2 sprints
- Block action items should be escalated immediately
- Dropped action items should be documented with reasoning

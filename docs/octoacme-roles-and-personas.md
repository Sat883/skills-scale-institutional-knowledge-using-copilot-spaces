# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Manager on scope, priorities, and roadmap alignment
- Works with Scrum Master to ensure smooth agile ceremonies and remove organizational impediments
- Coordinates with DevOps Engineer on release planning and deployment schedules
- Partners with Business Analyst on requirements gathering and stakeholder management

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and ensure adherence to agile practices. They serve the team by fostering a productive, collaborative environment and driving continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments that hinder team progress
- Coach the team on agile principles and best practices
- Shield the team from external distractions and context switching
- Track and improve team velocity and process metrics
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and flow
- Ensure agile practices are followed effectively
- Create an environment where the team can self-organize and thrive
- Drive continuous process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies facilitation
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Team health metrics and retrospective insights
- Coaching conversations with team members

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partners with Project Manager to align on timelines, risks, and cross-team dependencies
- Collaborates with Product Manager to ensure backlog readiness and clarity
- Works closely with Developers to understand and remove blockers
- Coordinates with QA/Testing to ensure quality practices are integrated into sprints

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create user-centric interfaces and experiences. They define user journeys, design intuitive interfaces, and ensure products are usable, accessible, and delightful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and create personas/user journeys
- Design wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Collaborate with Product Manager to validate design solutions
- Work with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation accuracy
- Support QA in usability testing and accessibility validation
- Maintain design systems and component libraries

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and aesthetically pleasing user experiences
- Ensure design decisions are grounded in user research and data
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction metrics

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design specs and handoff documentation
- Usability testing reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Manager on user needs, requirements, and priorities
- Partners with Developers during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Works with Business Analyst to understand business requirements and constraints
- Supports QA/Testing in validating usability and accessibility standards
- Coordinates with Scrum Master to participate in sprint planning and reviews

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers own deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and automation. They enable reliable, efficient software delivery through CI/CD practices and infrastructure as code.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure provisioning and configuration
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting systems
- Automate deployment and rollback processes
- Ensure security, scalability, and reliability of infrastructure
- Support incident response and disaster recovery

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and safe deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Improve developer productivity through automation
- Maintain secure and compliant infrastructure

### Typical Communication
- Deployment status updates and release coordination
- Infrastructure incident reports and post-mortems
- Automation roadmap and technical proposals
- Security and compliance updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Project Manager on release planning and deployment schedules
- Partners with Developers to streamline build and deployment workflows
- Works with QA/Testing to integrate automated testing into CI/CD pipelines
- Coordinates with Scrum Master on sprint deliverables related to infrastructure
- Supports Product Manager with deployment metrics and system health data

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather and refine requirements, bridge communication between business stakeholders and technical teams, and ensure project scope aligns with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into detailed user stories and acceptance criteria
- Analyze data and processes to identify improvement opportunities
- Maintain requirements traceability and documentation
- Facilitate requirement validation sessions with stakeholders
- Support backlog refinement and prioritization

### Goals
- Ensure clear, complete, and testable requirements
- Bridge communication gaps between business and technical teams
- Reduce scope creep and rework
- Improve alignment between project outcomes and business value

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user stories
- Stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Backlog refinement sessions
- Business impact analysis and reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Manager to define and prioritize requirements
- Collaborates with Project Manager on scope management and stakeholder communication
- Works with Developers to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- Supports UX/UI Designer with user research and business context
- Coordinates with QA/Testing to validate that acceptance criteria are met

---

## QA/Testing

### Role Summary
QA/Testing roles validate quality and ensure acceptance criteria are met. They catch defects early, verify usability, and maintain quality standards throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute test plans and test cases
- Perform functional, regression, and integration testing
- Conduct accessibility and usability testing
- Report and track defects with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria definition
- Support automation testing efforts

### Goals
- Ensure product quality meets defined standards
- Catch and prevent defects early in the lifecycle
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria
- Maintain high test coverage and effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Test reports and quality metrics
- Bug reports with reproduction steps
- Test plan reviews
- Sign-off on acceptance criteria

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Manager and Business Analyst to understand acceptance criteria
- Partners with Developers on testability and defect resolution
- Works with UX/UI Designer on usability and accessibility testing
- Supports DevOps Engineer in integrating automated tests into CI/CD
- Participates in sprint reviews with Scrum Master to demonstrate quality

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders provide input, approvals, and represent business interests. They guide strategic direction and ensure projects align with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic direction
- Review and approve key project decisions and deliverables
- Participate in project reviews and milestone checkpoints
- Communicate organizational priorities and constraints
- Champion project outcomes within the organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and align with strategy
- Provide timely feedback and approvals
- Support the team with resources and organizational alignment
- Drive adoption and success of project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and reviews
- Approval requests for major decisions
- Feedback sessions on demos and prototypes
- Strategic alignment discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Provides strategic input to Product Manager and Business Analyst
- Reviews progress with Project Manager and participates in milestone checkpoints
- Participates in sprint reviews facilitated by Scrum Master
- Offers feedback on design proposals from UX/UI Designer

---

## Role Collaboration Matrix

The following matrix summarizes key interactions and collaboration patterns:

| Role | Primary Collaborators | Key Touchpoints |
|------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **Product Manager** | Business Analyst, UX/UI Designer, Developers, Stakeholders | Backlog prioritization, requirements definition, sprint reviews |
| **Project Manager** | Scrum Master, DevOps Engineer, All roles | Planning, status updates, risk management, cross-team coordination |
| **Scrum Master** | Developers, QA/Testing, Product Manager, Project Manager | Sprint ceremonies, impediment removal, process improvements |
| **Developers** | UX/UI Designer, QA/Testing, DevOps Engineer, Scrum Master | Implementation, code reviews, testing, deployment |
| **QA/Testing** | Developers, Business Analyst, UX/UI Designer, DevOps Engineer | Test planning, acceptance criteria validation, quality gates |
| **UX/UI Designer** | Product Manager, Developers, Business Analyst, QA/Testing | Design reviews, user research, implementation support |
| **DevOps Engineer** | Developers, Project Manager, QA/Testing | CI/CD, deployments, infrastructure, monitoring |
| **Business Analyst** | Product Manager, Stakeholders, Developers, QA/Testing | Requirements gathering, backlog refinement, acceptance criteria |
| **Stakeholders** | Product Manager, Project Manager, Business Analyst | Strategic alignment, approvals, feedback |
